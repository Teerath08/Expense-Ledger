The Problem

Student money disappears
in small moments

Many students manage money for the first time when college brings new freedom and new expenses — without the tools to handle it responsibly.

01
☕

Impulse Spending

Snacks, cafés, and quick online purchases add up quietly. Before you know it, the budget is gone.

02
📊

No Clear Budget

Students may know their balance, but not what is actually safe to spend today or this week.

03
😱

Unplanned Costs

Travel, printing, subscriptions, and project costs can surprise students when they least expect it.

04
🎯

Saving Feels Difficult

A savings goal feels far away when spending isn't tracked. Goals keep getting pushed to next month.

Our Solution

One simple place to plan,
track, and improve

A student-first finance dashboard turns money management into a short, repeatable daily habit.

💰
Step 01

Set a Monthly Budget

Enter expected income and divide it into spending categories that match your lifestyle.

⚡
Step 02

Track Expenses Quickly

Log purchases in seconds and tag each one by category. No spreadsheets, no friction.

📈
Step 03

See What's Left

A live dashboard shows budget used and remaining money at a glance — always up to date.

🏆
Step 04

Save Toward a Goal

Set a target amount and track progress in small, satisfying steps. Watch it grow.

🔔
Step 05

Get Gentle Alerts

Receive a supportive nudge when a category is close to its limit — not a shame spiral.

🔍
Step 06

Review Your Habits

A monthly summary highlights spending patterns without judgement, so you can improve next month.

How It Works

A simple 4-step journey

Designed to fit into a student's routine — no complicated spreadsheets required.

🚀
1

Start

Enter your monthly allowance, income, and fixed expenses to get your baseline.

📋
2

Plan

Set category limits for food, travel, shopping, and more that feel realistic for you.

📝
3

Track

Add an expense whenever you spend — the dashboard updates instantly in real time.

💡
4

Improve

Use alerts and monthly summaries to make smarter decisions next month.

Design Principle
"Make the next good money decision obvious — not overwhelming."

Responsible Design

Money tools should feel safe,
private, and non-judgmental

Trust is part of the product — especially when people are entering personal financial information.

🔒

Privacy First

We only collect what the prototype needs and explain how it's used. No tracking, no selling, no surprises.

🎛️

User Control

Students can edit or delete their entries and budgets at any time. Full ownership of their own data.

💬

No Shame Language

We use supportive, encouraging reminders rather than guilt or blame. Progress over perfection, always.

🎯

Clear Boundaries

We show spending insights, not risky promises or guaranteed outcomes. Honest, transparent, clear.
